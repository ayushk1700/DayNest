# DayNest intellectual-property review · 20 October 2026

## Conclusion

**Not cleared for public store submission under the current name/design.** This is a practical source/provenance and public-web review, not a legal opinion, a finding of infringement, a litigation search or comprehensive trademark clearance. Actual claims depend on rights, jurisdiction, similarity and facts not established here. No product, logo, website audience or store package was changed by this review.

The owner confirmed today that (1) they took the sunflower reference photograph themselves and (2) the UI reference screenshots came from another product. Those are user statements, not independently verified ownership records.

## Public name matches observed

1. **Daynest**, Apple App Store, app ID 6745919101: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/daynest/id6745919101
   The listing describes managing daily and weekly team tasks. This is directly relevant software/productivity use; it is not proof of registered trademark rights.
2. **DayNest**, smart family displays/planning: https://daynest.com.au/
   The website describes family calendars, chores and routines. Its existence is an additional clearance lead, not a finding about rights in every country.

No registered-mark status or date of first use was established. Search engines and app stores do not substitute for registry/common-law clearance. Search exact and similar spellings, phonetics, logos and related goods/services in intended territories. Relevant official starting points:

- IP India search guidance: https://www.ipindia.gov.in/trade-marks-before-you-apply-search-existing-trademarks
- USPTO comprehensive clearance guidance: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks

## Before submission

1. Resolve name clearance or rename after searching the proposed replacement. A new spelling or extra word is not automatically safe.
2. Identify the other product used for screenshot references and compare the actual current dashboard/skins with those screenshots. Independently redesign distinctive expression or obtain rights. Keep the common useful functions.
3. Keep the owner's original sunflower photo and provenance records. Do not present AI generation as a guarantee of originality or exclusive rights.
4. Keep MIT/Flaticon notices and credits in every final package. Review attribution on the product website/store materials as well as inside Settings.
5. Resolve Open-Meteo distribution terms; do not expose a paid provider key in the client.
6. Have an IP professional review the concrete name/design findings for intended launch territories before investing in a public-store launch. No app-store submission was made as part of this check.
