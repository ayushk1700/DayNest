# Wellbeing privacy and support

Wellbeing recording is off by default. In View stats, choose Enable local recording to start retaining DayNest timer-running intervals and work/break completion events in this browser profile. Recording can be paused and resumed. It does not read browsing history, other tabs, other applications or device-wide activity, and adds no network analytics or browser permissions. Existing habit dates and legacy work-completion counts were already stored by their widgets; they remain separate from optional duration recording.

Running timers remain timestamp-based across page closure and browser restart. An enabled recording session ends at its scheduled deadline even when the worker wakes later. These totals describe timer session time, including overlapping timers. They do not establish unique attention, screen time or real-world rest. Unrecorded or paused periods are not reconstructed. Timezone changes can move retained timestamps between displayed calendar days.

Retention is 30 or 90 rolling days, bounded further by 1,000 intervals, 1,000 completion events and the shared document-size limit. Capacity-related removal is disclosed in the page. Retention cleanup occurs on dashboard activity/background reconciliation; an unopened installation can retain expired records until it next runs. Reducing retention removes older timer records. Clearing Wellbeing history removes its intervals and completion events, keeps goals/preferences and other widgets, and starts fresh immediately if recording remains enabled. Clearing is outside undo. Deleting a Wellbeing card keeps the shared history. Dashboard reset replaces the shared settings/history with the sample dashboard, after making the existing recovery copy.

JSON backups include retained history and snapshot running progress up to export time. They are unencrypted files under the user's control. Import/restore pauses recording and running timers, removes in-progress recording markers and suppresses replay of past notifications. Resume recording explicitly after checking the imported data. Existing recovery copies, downloaded backups and operating-system/profile backups can retain previously cleared data. Remove those copies separately if desired.

If minutes are missing, check recording status, start date, the selected period/timezone, retention and capacity warnings. Existing completion counts do not contain durations. The daily average divides retained recorded focus time by all selected calendar days. Habit targets are unavailable, so no completion percentage is invented. A break ring appears only after setting a real daily break-timer goal. The focus-window summary needs three recorded days and at least one recorded hour and identifies the two-hour interval with the largest retained total; it is descriptive rather than a prediction.
